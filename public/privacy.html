Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lekalu handles information when you use the Lekalu personal finance and expense tracking app.

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the developer using the developer contact email shown on Lekalu's Google Play listing.

Information We Collect

Lekalu may collect or store the following information, depending on the features you use:

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication user ID.
  • Google profile information, such as email address, display name, and profile photo, if you use Google sign-in on a supported version of the app or web app.
  • Expense and finance information that you enter, such as activities, amounts, descriptions, bank or source names, trackables, tracker status, and group expense information.
  • App preferences, such as theme settings and analytics display preferences.
  • Biometric credential metadata stored on your device if you enable biometric verification. Lekalu does not receive your fingerprint or face data.
  • Technical information handled by Firebase and Google services, such as authentication tokens, security logs, device or browser information, and service diagnostics.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and maintain Lekalu's core features, including:

  • Creating and signing in to your account.
  • Saving and syncing your expense tracking data.
  • Supporting group expense sharing features.
  • Applying your app preferences across sessions.
  • Protecting accounts, preventing abuse, and diagnosing service issues.

Sharing and Third Parties

Lekalu uses Firebase and Google services for authentication, database storage, hosting, and related infrastructure. Your information may be processed by those services as needed to provide the app. We do not sell your personal information.

If you join or create a group, expense information associated with that group may be visible to other members of the same group as part of the app's group sharing functionality.

Security

Lekalu uses Firebase services and HTTPS-backed connections for data transfer. Some sensitive app data may be encrypted before storage. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we use reasonable safeguards appropriate for the app's features.

Data Retention and Deletion

Lekalu keeps account and app data for as long as needed to provide the service, unless you delete it or request deletion. You can clear app data from within the app where available. To request account deletion or deletion of associated cloud data, use the developer contact email shown on Lekalu's Google Play listing.

Children

Lekalu is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Lekalu, contact the developer using the Google Play listing contact email so the information can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
