Account Deletion

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Lekalu users can request deletion of their account and related app data. To submit a request, contact the developer using the developer contact email shown on Lekalu's Google Play listing.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Open Lekalu's Google Play listing.
  2. Find the developer contact email listed for the app.
  3. Send an email with the subject "Lekalu Account Deletion Request".
  4. Include the email address associated with your Lekalu account.

What Will Be Deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete account-related data associated with your Lekalu account where applicable, including:

  • Your Lekalu account record and authentication-related profile information.
  • Expense tracking data you entered, such as activities, amounts, descriptions, bank or source names, trackables, tracker status, and app preferences.
  • Group membership references associated with your account.

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained if required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, backup recovery, or to protect other users' group records. If information is retained, it will be kept only as long as necessary for those purposes.

Processing Time

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days. We may ask for additional information if needed to verify account ownership.
